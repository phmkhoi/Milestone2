\section{Conclusion}
\vspace{-1ex}
We present \modelns, a novel approach for robust molecular conformer generation that combines an SE(3)-equivariant hierarchical VAE with geometric coarse-graining techniques for autoregressive conformer generation. By utilizing easy-to-obtain approximate conformations, our model effectively learns the optimal distortion to generate low-energy conformers. Furthermore, \model possesses unrestricted degrees of freedom, as it can adjust atomic coordinates, distances, and torsion angles freely. \modelns's CG procedure can also be tailored to handle even larger systems, whereas prior methods are restricted to full FG or torsion angle space.
Our experiments demonstrate the effectiveness of \model compared to existing methods, requiring 92.7\% less data and 2\% of the training iterations in the most difficult GEOM-DRUGS task. Moreover, our study expands upon existing conformer generation benchmarks, providing valuable insights into robust generation.
