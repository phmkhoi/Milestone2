\section{Conclusion}
\vspace{-2ex}
We present \modelns, a novel approach for robust molecular conformer generation that combines an SE(3)-equivariant hierarchical VAE with geometric coarse-graining techniques for accurate conformer generation. By utilizing easy-to-obtain approximate conformations, our model effectively learns the optimal distortion to generate low-energy conformers. \model possesses unrestricted degrees of freedom, as it can adjust atomic coordinates, distances, and torsion angles freely. \modelns's CG procedure can also be tailored to handle even larger systems, whereas prior methods are restricted to full FG or torsion angle space.
Our experiments demonstrate the effectiveness of \model compared to existing methods. Our study also extends recent 3D molecule-protein benchmarks to conformer generation, providing valuable insights into robust generation and downstream biologically relevant tasks.
