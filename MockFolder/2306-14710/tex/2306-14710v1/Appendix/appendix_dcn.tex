    \caption{Moment maps (moment 0, 1,  and 2 in the left, centre, and right panels, respectively) of DCN (3-2) emission towards the protoclusters G008.67 (top), G010.62 (middle), and G012.80 (bottom). All three moments have been determined over a velocity range of 31.7 to 43~\kms, $-9.3$ to 5.8~\kms, and 30.7 to 43~\kms for G008.67, G010.62, and G012.80, respectively.
