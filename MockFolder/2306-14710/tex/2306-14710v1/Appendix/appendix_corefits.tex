\section{DCN line fits and spectra}
\label{appendix:spectral}
We show here the full set of Single- and Complex-type DCN (3-2) spectra for each of the 15 protoclusters in the ALMA-IMF survey. We also provide the Tables of the associated core number, core name, position, and major and minor axes taken from \citet{louvet22} along with the DCN line fits extracted here for each of the respective cores. If no fit parameters are provided for the DCN or if the name Single or Complex is not provided, the core was not detected in DCN.
