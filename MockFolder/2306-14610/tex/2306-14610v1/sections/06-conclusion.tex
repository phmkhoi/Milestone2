\section{Conclusion}

Our investigation reveals significant biases present in existing benchmarks for the compositional comprehension capability of vision-language models. The severity of this vulnerability is exemplified by text-only models without access to the image outperforming vision-language models. To address this, we introduce \method, a novel benchmark for evaluating the compositionality of vision-language understanding. Unlike previous benchmarks that relied on rule-based templates, we leverage large language models to generate less biased negatives and employ adversarial filtering mechanisms to minimize biases. Through reassessment of state-of-the-art models and recently proposed compositionality inducing mechanisms, we uncover a significant overestimation of their advancements, underscoring the need for further innovation.
