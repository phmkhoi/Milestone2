\begin{abstract}
  % 1 background - large model and multi-modal
  Though the advancement of pre-trained large language models unfolds, the \ct{exploration} of \ct{building} a unified model for language and other multimodal data, such as motion, \ct{remains challenging and untouched so far.} %presents a significant challenge.
  %
  % 2 Insight/observation - motion task small task,
  % A uniform pre-trained motion framework has the potential to extend to numerous motion-relevant tasks, despite the myriad challenges and hurdles faced.
  % 
  % 3 Propose/motivation - motion as body language, semantic information as words in the language
  Fortunately, human motion displays a semantic coupling akin to human language, often perceived as a form of body language.
  %
  By fusing language data with \ct{large-scale motion} models,% of a significantly larger scale compared to motion data, 
  motion-language pre-training that can enhance the performance of motion-related tasks becomes feasible. %and also \ct{} establish a more versatile and user-friendly framework.
  % 
  % 4 method - propose MotionGPT
  Driven by this insight, we propose MotionGPT, a unified, \ct{versatile, and user-friendly} motion-language model to handle \ct{multiple} motion-relevant tasks.
  %     
  % 5 details - specifically, motion quaternization xx, language backbone xx
  Specifically, we employ the discrete vector quantization for human motion and transfer 3D motion into motion tokens, similar to \ct{the generation process of} word tokens. 
  %
  Building upon this ``motion vocabulary'', we perform language modeling on both motion and text in a unified manner, treating human motion as a specific language.
  % 
  Moreover, inspired by prompt learning, we pre-train MotionGPT with a mixture of motion-language data and fine-tune it on prompt-based question-and-answer tasks.
  %
  % 6 Experimental results - xx
  Extensive experiments demonstrate that MotionGPT achieves state-of-the-art performances on multiple motion tasks including text-driven motion generation, motion captioning, motion prediction, and motion in-between.
  
\end{abstract}
